# PART 8 — LEAD SCORING SYSTEM

## 8.1 Lead Score Tiers

### 🔥 HOT — "Actively shopping + strong profile + ready to talk"

**Definition:** Within 30-60 days of buying, has financial readiness, has booked or is booking a call.

**Indicators:**
- Timeline: Within 30 days or 1-3 months
- Strong or Workable pillars across the board
- Income supports target price
- Has booked an appointment
- Provided full lead info (name, email, phone, ZIP)
- 680+ credit
- Down payment ≥ 5%
- DTI workable

**Action:**
- Personal phone call within 1 business hour (during business hours)
- If after hours, call first thing next morning
- Personalized email follow-up same day
- Pre-call research: review their questionnaire answers, prepare scenarios
- Goal: pre-approval within 7 days

---

### 🟠 WARM — "Likely borrower but has an obstacle"

**Definition:** Interested, has some readiness, but a clear obstacle needs addressing.

**Indicators:**
- Timeline: 1-6 months
- 1-2 pillars are Tight or Likely Obstacle
- Has provided full lead info or is on email nurture
- Generally stable income
- 620-720 credit
- Some down payment

**Action:**
- Personal email within 4 business hours
- Educational follow-up about their specific obstacle
- Offer free call to discuss
- If responsive, move toward appointment
- If unresponsive, nurture sequence

---

### 🟡 NURTURE — "Needs credit/debt/income improvement"

**Definition:** Has the desire but profile needs 3-12 months of work before ready.

**Indicators:**
- Multiple Tight or Likely Obstacle pillars
- Credit below 620, recent BK/foreclosure
- High DTI (above 50%)
- Insufficient down payment
- Timeline: 6-12 months or "researching"

**Action:**
- Add to long-term nurture sequence (monthly)
- Educational content about credit improvement, DTI reduction
- Re-check in 60-90 days
- Don't push for immediate appointment
- Goal: get them ready in 6-12 months

---

### 🔵 FUTURE BUYER — "Not ready now but may become ready"

**Definition:** 12+ months out, exploratory, may be first-time buyer doing research.

**Indicators:**
- Timeline: 6-12+ months or "researching"
- Just exploring
- Multiple "not sure" answers
- Provided email but not phone
- Educational intent

**Action:**
- Long-term nurture
- Educational content
- Quarterly check-in
- "First-time buyer" content
- Goal: build relationship for when they're ready

---

### ⚪ LOW INTENT — "Primarily educational / research"

**Definition:** Just browsing, doing research, won't be in market soon.

**Indicators:**
- Just exploring
- "Not sure" on most things
- No contact info provided
- No appointment attempt

**Action:**
- Minimal effort
- Continue to serve SEO and brand value
- No active outreach
- May convert later via remarketing

---

## 8.2 Lead Score Calculation

A numerical score (0-100) supplements the tier system:

### Scoring weights

| Factor | Max Points | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Timeline** | 25 | Within 30 days = 25, 1-3 months = 20, 3-6 = 15, 6-12 = 10, researching = 5 |
| **Profile strength** | 30 | Based on pillar scores (Strong +5, Workable +3, Tight +1, Obstacle 0) |
| **Lead capture depth** | 20 | Email only = 5, Full info = 15, Booked appointment = 20 |
| **Credit score** | 10 | 760+ = 10, 720-759 = 8, 680-719 = 6, 620-679 = 4, <620 = 2, not sure = 3 |
| **Down payment %** | 10 | 20%+ = 10, 10-19% = 8, 5-9% = 6, 3-4% = 4, <3% = 2 |
| **Engagement** | 5 | (engagement scoring from email/SMS behavior) |

### Tier mapping

| Score | Tier |
|---|---|
| 80-100 | 🔥 HOT |
| 60-79 | 🟠 WARM |
| 40-59 | 🟡 NURTURE |
| 20-39 | 🔵 FUTURE BUYER |
| 0-19 | ⚪ LOW INTENT |

## 8.4 Lead Routing

### When to call
- 🔥 HOT: Within 1 business hour during business hours; first thing next morning otherwise
- 🟠 WARM: Same business day
- 🟡 NURTURE: Add to sequence, no immediate call
- 🔵 FUTURE: Add to long-term sequence
- ⚪ LOW: No action

### How to call
- **Phone first** for HOT (per TCPA consent)
- **Email first** for WARM
- **Never** for NURTURE/FUTURE/LOW (let sequence do its work)

### What to say
- Reference their specific situation (their primary obstacle)
- Don't be salesy
- Be the trusted advisor
- Offer a free, no-pressure conversation
- Have a calendar link ready

## 8.5 Lead Quality Metrics (Track Over Time)

### Per-source metrics
- CPL (cost per lead) by source
- Lead-to-appointment conversion by source
- Lead-to-funded by source
- Average loan size by source
- Average revenue per lead by source

### Per-tier metrics
- Appointment show rate by tier
- Application rate by tier
- Funded rate by tier
- Time from lead to funded by tier

### Diagnostic data
- Average pillar scores by lead source
- Most common primary obstacles
- Most common loan program recommendations
- Geographic distribution of leads

## 8.6 Lead Retention & Compliance

- **Retention period:** 7 years (mortgage industry norm, but verify with attorney)
- **Secure storage:** Encrypted at rest, encrypted in transit
- **Access controls:** Only you (and any staff with need-to-know)
- **CCPA compliance:** Honor "do not sell" requests; data deletion on request
- **Audit trail:** Log every access, every change
- **Backups:** Encrypted, off-site, tested recovery
- **Disposal:** Secure deletion after retention period
