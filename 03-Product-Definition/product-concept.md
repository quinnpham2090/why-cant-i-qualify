# PART 3 — PRODUCT DEFINITION

## 3.1 Product Concept

**Working Name:** "Why Can't I Qualify?" (placeholder — see Part 16 for brand recommendation)

**One-line description:** An interactive mortgage qualification diagnostic that helps U.S. consumers understand *what may be* holding them back from qualifying for a mortgage — and *what potential steps* could improve their situation — then connects qualified consumers with a licensed mortgage professional.

**Type of product:** Consumer mortgage problem-diagnosis and education tool that generates highly qualified mortgage leads.

## 3.2 What This Product Is (and Is Not)

### This product IS:
- A **preliminary educational assessment** of mortgage readiness
- A **consumer mortgage problem-diagnosis tool** that helps people self-identify likely obstacles
- An **educational resource** that explains mortgage qualification concepts in plain English
- A **lead-generation funnel** that connects motivated consumers with a licensed MLO
- A **content hub** (SEO) for mortgage qualification topics

### This product IS NOT:
- Not a lender
- Not a creditor.
- Not an underwriter.
- Not a loan approval system.
- Not a denial system.
- Not a preapproval.
- Not a loan commitment.
- Not a guarantee of qualification.
- Not a substitute for a licensed loan officer.
- Not a credit decision.

## 3.3 Core Value Proposition

**For consumers:** "Find out what may be holding you back from qualifying for the mortgage you want — and what potential steps could improve your situation — in 5 minutes, anonymously, with no credit pull."

**For the MLO (you):** "Receive pre-qualified, motivated borrowers who already understand their likely obstacle and are ready to talk to a licensed professional."

## 3.4 Information Collected (Preliminary Set)

### Required inputs (kept minimal to maximize completion):

| # | Field | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **State** | Location-specific loan programs, licensing, regional cost differences |
| 2 | **Purpose** | Purchase / Refinance / Cash-out refi / Just exploring |
| 3 | **Property type** | Single family / Condo / Townhome / Multi-family / Manufactured |
| 4 | **Estimated purchase price (or current value if refi)** | Anchors the analysis |
| 5 | **Annual household income (gross)** | Drives DTI and qualification |
| 6 | **Employment type** | W-2 / Self-employed / Commission / Variable / Retired / Other |
| 7 | **Approximate credit score** | Band: 760+ / 720-759 / 680-719 / 620-679 / 580-619 / below 580 / not sure |
| 8 | **Approximate total monthly debt payments** | Drives DTI; includes auto, student loans, credit cards min, child support, etc. |
| 9 | **Available down payment (or cash to bring to refi)** | Cash readiness |
| 10 | **HOA?** (if condo/townhome) | Property readiness |
| 11 | **First-time buyer?** | Eligibility for special programs |
| 12 | **Timeline** | Within 30 days / 1-3 months / 3-6 months / 6-12 months / Just researching |

### Optional/conditional inputs (asked only when relevant):
- Self-employed → years in business, type of business, last 2 years tax return status
- High debt → specific debt breakdown
- Property is condo → HOA dues, HOA certification status, complex project type
- Looking at purchase in specific city/ZIP → cost adjustment

### Information NOT collected initially:
- Full SSN
- Date of birth
- Full address
- Bank account numbers
- Exact credit score (only band)
- Employer name
- W-2 or pay stub uploads (would happen later with the LO)

This keeps the barrier low and respects privacy.

## 3.5 Output (Educational Assessment)

The diagnostic produces a structured report with:

### A. **Estimated Purchase Range** (not a guaranteed amount)
- Conservative / Likely / Stretch scenarios
- E.g., "Estimated comfortable range: $280,000 – $340,000 based on the income and debt you provided. Actual qualification depends on a lender's full review."

### B. **Primary Potential Obstacle**
- E.g., "Debt-to-income ratio may be the primary area requiring review."

### C. **Secondary Potential Obstacles**
- E.g., "Cash-to-close may be tight given the down payment you provided."

### D. **Potential Strengths**
- E.g., "Your credit profile may be workable for many loan programs."
- E.g., "Income documentation for W-2 borrowers is typically straightforward."

### E. **Estimated Monthly Payment (PITI)**
- For the target purchase price at current rates
- Principal & Interest + estimated Taxes + Insurance + (HOA) + (PMI/MIP)

### F. **Estimated DTI (Back-end)**
- Monthly PITI + other debts / gross monthly income

### G. **Potential Next Steps** (educational)
1. Review specific debt items
2. Review income documentation
3. Adjust target price or down payment
4. Explore alternative loan programs
5. Speak with a licensed mortgage professional

### H. **What a Licensed Loan Officer Would Need**
- Educational list of documents the LO will request
- Sets expectations without causing friction

## 3.6 Forbidden Wording (Compliance Risk)

The system must NEVER say or imply:

| ❌ Never say | ✅ Say instead |
|---|---|
| "You're approved." | "This is an educational estimate, not an approval." |
| "You qualify for $X." | "You may potentially qualify for amounts in the $X–$Y range." |
| "Guaranteed approval." | "Mortgage qualification depends on a full lender review." |
| "You'll be denied." | "This may be an area requiring review with a licensed professional." |
| "We approve loans." | "We provide a preliminary educational assessment." |
| "We can get you approved." | "A licensed professional can review your specific situation." |
| "You're pre-approved." | "Pre-approval requires a full application with a lender." |
| "Bad credit OK." | "Multiple loan programs may exist for various credit profiles." |
| "Low credit? No problem." | "Loan options vary by lender and program." |
| "AI-approved." | "Preliminary, automated educational estimate." |
| "AI denied." | "The automated review identified areas that may need attention." |

## 3.7 Required Disclosures (Always Visible)

The diagnostic must clearly and prominently state:

1. **"This is a preliminary educational assessment, not a mortgage application, preapproval, or commitment to lend. Actual qualification requires a full lender review."**

2. **"Your information is not shared with multiple lenders. By submitting your contact information, you consent to be contacted by [Business Name], a licensed mortgage professional (NMLS #XXXXXX)."**

3. **"Equal Housing Opportunity. [Business Name] is licensed in [state(s)]."**

4. **"We do not perform a hard credit inquiry. The credit range you provide is your own estimate."**

5. Privacy policy link
6. Terms of use link
7. TCPA / SMS consent language (if SMS is used)

## 3.8 Product Positioning

### Where this fits in the market:

```
[Anonymous / Educational]  →  [Prequal (soft) ]  →  [Preapproval (hard) ]  →  [Application ]  →  [Underwriting ]  →  [Closing ]
       ▲
   WE LIVE HERE
```

Most mortgage sites jump straight to a "prequalification" form, which feels invasive. This product lives in the **anonymous / educational zone** where consumers are 10-50x more numerous than those willing to apply.

## 3.9 Differentiation

| Existing | This product |
|---|---|
| "Get pre-approved in 3 minutes" — requires SSN, hard credit | "See what may be holding you back" — anonymous, no SSN, no credit pull |
| Single number ("$350,000") | Range with obstacles and strengths |
| Generic education | Personalized diagnosis |
| Lead form first | Diagnosis first, lead capture second |
| One-size-fits-all | Conditional logic, scenario-specific advice |
| No follow-up education | Educational follow-up sequence |
| Aggressive sales tone | Calming, problem-solving tone |

## 3.10 Three Audiences the Product Will Serve

1. **The Worried First-Time Buyer** — doesn't know if they qualify, anxious, no idea about credit/DTI
2. **The Recently Denied Borrower** — was just told "no" and doesn't know why or what to do
3. **The Self-Employed / Gig Worker** — frustrated that traditional calculators don't account for their income
4. (secondary) **The "Just Looking" Researcher** — early-funnel SEO traffic, future lead
5. (secondary) **The Refi Hopper** — wants to know if refi is realistic

The core primary audience is **#1, #2, and #3** — the ones with the most acute pain and highest intent.
