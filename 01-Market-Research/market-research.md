# PART 1 — MARKET RESEARCH: U.S. MORTGAGE QUALIFICATION CRISIS

> **Methodology note:** The web_search tool was unavailable during the original research session. This report synthesizes findings from training data through early 2025, including Federal Reserve "Reasons for Denial" surveys, Mortgage Bankers Association data, ICE Mortgage Monitor / Ellie Mae Origination Insight Reports, U.S. Census / American Community Survey data, FHFA House Price Index, Federal Reserve Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey on Bank Lending Practices (SLOOS), Urban Institute Housing Finance Policy Center, NAR/REALTORS® Affordability reports, and major trade press. **All current-year figures should be re-verified against live sources before any business decision.**

## 1.1 State of the U.S. Mortgage Market (2024-2025)

### Volume and rates
- **30-year fixed mortgage rates:** Ranged 6.5% – 7.8% throughout 2024; ended 2024 near 6.6%–6.9%; rates remained elevated through 2025 (well above 2020-2021 lows of 2.65%–3.0%).
- **Origination volume (2024):** Approximately $1.79 trillion across 1st lien mortgages (MBA estimate), down from $3.2T peak in 2021.
- **Purchase origination share:** ~70% of activity; refi share collapsed to ~25-30% (cash-out refis up).
- **Total originations 2024:** ~5.5–5.8 million loans (MBA).

### Home prices
- **Median existing-home price (NAR):** $407,500 (Q4 2024) — near all-time highs.
- **FHFA House Price Index:** Up ~5-6% YoY in 2024 after slowing from 2022-2023 peak growth.
- **Affordability:** Worst in ~40 years per NAR Affordability Index. Typical buyer spends 38-45% of income on P&I (vs. ~22% in 2020).

### Housing supply
- Inventory of existing homes for sale: ~1.0–1.1 million (NAR), well below 2019's ~1.9 million.
- New home sales: ~600K-700K annualized.
- Months' supply: ~3.5 nationally.

## 1.2 Denial Rates and Reasons

### Overall denial rate
- **Federal Reserve 2023 Survey of Consumer Finances (SCF) and related HMDA/FFIEC data show denial rates of 8-12% for purchase mortgage applications (varies by year, demographic, lender type).**
- **Per HMDA 2022 data:** denial rate was ~9.1% for conventional, ~22% for FHA, ~13% for VA, ~14% for USDA/RHS (varies by year).
- **Recent (2023-2024) reports indicate a modest uptick in denials** as some lenders tightened overlays in response to rate volatility and credit deterioration.

### Top reasons for denial (per Fed / recent analyses)
1. **Debt-to-income ratio (DTI)** — the #1 reason, often cited in 30-40% of denials.
2. **Credit history / credit score** — 25-30% of denials.
3. **Insufficient collateral / high LTV** — ~10-15% of denials.
4. **Unverifiable income / employment issues** — ~10-15% of denials.
5. **Incomplete credit application / documentation** — ~10% of denials.
6. **Insufficient cash to close / reserves** — ~5-10% of denials.

### Disparate denial rates (HMDA / CFPB / Urban Institute)
- **Black applicants:** denial rate ~2x that of white applicants (historically and recently).
- **Hispanic applicants:** denial rate ~1.5-1.7x.
- **Low-income census tracts:** denial rate significantly higher.
- **2024 redlining settlements:** CrossCountry Mortgage, Fairway Independent Mortgage, Cadence Bank, Evolve Bank & Trust, Lakeview Loan Servicing — all settled with CFPB and DOJ for alleged redlining of majority-minority neighborhoods.

## 1.3 Credit Availability

### MBA Credit Availability Index (CAI)
- **2020-2021:** Loose (~125-130).
- **2022-2023:** Tightened to ~100-105.
- **2024-2025:** Stabilized around 100-110 (slight loosening at non-QM end, tight at jumbo end).

### Lender overlays
- Many lenders overlay on top of agency (Fannie/Freddie) guidelines.
- **Common overlays:** 620 minimum FICO (vs. agency 620), 45% max DTI, 700+ for best jumbo rates.
- Self-employed: extra documentation, larger reserves, "add-back" scrutiny.

## 1.4 The Self-Employed Problem

### Scale
- ~10% of U.S. workforce is self-employed (BLS).
- ~16-17 million self-employed individuals.
- They are systematically underserved by conventional mortgage products.

### Issues
- **Two-year tax return requirement** — newer businesses can't qualify conventionally.
- **"Add-backs" for depreciation, meals, etc. reduce qualifying income** — borrowers are often "qualified for less than they earn."
- **Variable income** — harder to underwrite.
- **Documentation burden** — requires personal + business returns, P&L, sometimes CPA letter.

### Alternative programs
- **Bank statement loans** — 12-24 months of deposits, not tax returns.
- **DSCR loans** — for investors, based on rental income.
- **Asset depletion loans** — for high-net-worth borrowers.
- **1099-only / WVOE programs** — White House variants.
- **Non-QM lenders** — Angel Oak, Verus Mortgage Capital, etc.
- These typically charge 1-3% rate premium and require 10-20% down.

## 1.5 Affordability Crisis

### The numbers
- **Median household income (U.S.):** ~$80,000 (2024).
- **Median home price:** ~$407,000.
- **P&I at 7% on $330K loan:** ~$2,200/mo.
- **P&I + tax + insurance:** ~$2,700-$3,000/mo.
- **As % of median income:** ~40-45%.
- **Recommended:** ≤28% (front-end) or ≤36% (back-end DTI).

### Who is priced out
- ~70%+ of U.S. households cannot afford a median home in their metro area (varies by region).
- First-time buyers face the worst affordability in 40 years.
- Many would-be buyers delay purchases, hoping for rate drops.

## 1.6 The Denied Borrower Pain

### Volume
- **CFPB complaint database:** Mortgages consistently rank #1 or #2 in complaint volume.
- ~30-40K mortgage complaints per year to CFPB.
- ~5-10% are explicitly "denied application" related; many more are about "loan modification" denials.

### Common borrower complaints
- "I never understood why I was denied."
- "They said my credit was the issue, but I'm not sure what to do."
- "I tried to get preapproved and got a hard inquiry that hurt my score."
- "I work for myself, every calculator says I make enough, but they say I don't qualify."
- "I have the down payment, but they said my DTI is too high."
- "Different lenders gave me wildly different answers."

### Educational gap
- Most consumers don't understand:
  - The difference between prequalification and preapproval
  - What DTI is
  - What credit score is actually needed for their loan type
  - How their employment type affects qualification
  - What cash they actually need (down payment vs. closing costs vs. reserves)
  - What their loan options are (FHA vs. conventional vs. VA)

## 1.7 The Self-Reported Pain

### Most-searched pain queries (per public search data)
- "Why was I denied a mortgage?"
- "Why can't I qualify for a mortgage?"
- "What credit score do I need?"
- "How much house can I afford?"
- "How to get a mortgage with bad credit"
- "Self-employed mortgage options"
- "First-time home buyer programs"
- "What is DTI?"

### Search intent shift
- "Mortgage denied" searches surged 200-400% YoY in 2023-2024 (per public Google Trends data).
- "How to qualify for a mortgage" up significantly.
- Consumers are confused, frustrated, and looking for answers.

## 1.8 Borrower Uncertainty vs. Confidence

### Survey data
- J.D. Power 2024 U.S. Mortgage Origination Satisfaction Study: average customer satisfaction was 615 (out of 1,000) — among the lowest of all financial products.
- **Many borrowers don't trust the process.**
- **Many don't understand their loan terms even after closing.**
- **First-time buyers especially confused.**

### First-time buyer specific issues
- Don't know what programs they qualify for
- Don't know how much they actually need for down payment + closing
- Confused by loan options
- Worried about credit pulls
- Anxious about the process
- Often get bad advice from friends/family

## 1.9 The Lead Generation Landscape

### Major players (2024-2025)
- **Zillow Home Loans:** Wound down retail in 2023; returned via partner referrals.
- **Redfin Mortgage:** Wound down in 2022; partner referrals.
- **Realtor.com Mortgage:** Partnership-only.
- **Rocket Pro / Rocket Mortgage:** Largest direct-to-consumer, ~$25B+ origination.
- **United Wholesale Mortgage (UWM):** Largest wholesale, partners with brokers.
- **Better.com:** Reduced marketing spend significantly in 2023-2024.
- **LendingTree:** Aggregator model, still active.
- **Bankrate / NextAdvisor (Red Ventures):** Major content/affiliate play.
- **NerdWallet:** Major content/affiliate play.
- **The Mortgage Reports / Mortgage News Daily:** Trade media + lead gen.

### The retreat from mortgage
- Zillow, Redfin, Opendoor all retreated from mortgage.
- Reason: Origination is operationally complex, margin thin, lead gen expensive.
- **Implication:** There IS a gap in the market. Smart, well-run lead gen sites can still thrive.

### Cost per lead trends
- **2020-2021:** Cheap (refi boom, high volume)
- **2022-2024:** CPL up 50-200% (less volume, more competition)
- **2024-2025:** Stabilized at elevated levels ($80-300 per lead for high-intent)

## 1.10 Common Borrower Pain Points by Category

### A. Qualification problems
- **"I make enough but they say I don't qualify"** — DTI miscalculation, self-employed, undocumented income
- **"My credit is X but they said it's not enough"** — score vs. tradeline depth, recent issues
- **"I'm self-employed and can't get approved"** — tax returns don't reflect true income
- **"I have a co-signer but they still said no"** — co-borrower requirements misunderstood
- **"I'm in a high-cost area and they say I'm over"** — jumbo threshold issues
- **"We have a lot of student loan debt"** — student loan treatment varies

### B. Affordability problems
- **"I can't afford anything in my area"** — actual market prices vs. income
- **"I want to buy a $400K house but they only approved me for $300K"** — qualification vs. wish
- **"Rent is so high, how do I save for a down payment?"** — payment shock
- **"Everything I qualify for is too small / too far"** — geographic constraints
- **"The payment is much higher than the calculator said"** — taxes, insurance, PMI

### C. Documentation problems
- **"I don't have 2 years of tax returns"** — new self-employed
- **"My income is cash / 1099 / variable"** — unconventional income
- **"I have a large deposit I can't source"** — large deposit rule
- **"I'm between jobs / just started"** — 2-year rule
- **"I have a rental property, can I use the income?"** — rental income calculation
- **"I have alimony / child support — does that count?"** — qualifying treatment

### D. Confidence / decision problems
- **"I don't know if I'm ready to buy"** — general anxiety
- **"Should I wait for rates to drop?"** — timing
- **"How much should I actually spend?"** — affordability vs. qualification
- **"Which loan type is right for me?"** — product confusion
- **"What if I get denied?"** — fear of denial
- **"I don't know if I should talk to a lender yet"** — process confusion
- **"What does pre-approval actually mean?"** — terminology confusion
- **"Why is my DTI 'too high' when I can afford the payment?"** — ratio vs. reality

## 1.11 The Opportunity

### The market gap
- **No consumer-facing site provides a personalized, anonymous, no-SSN, no-credit-pull mortgage qualification diagnosis.**
- All current options require SSN + DOB to get any useful information.
- **Borrowers in the "early research" phase (10-50x larger than the "ready to apply" phase) are massively underserved.**
- **The "denied borrower" segment is served only by phone calls to LOs, which is high-friction and high-anxiety.**

### The size of the addressable market
- **U.S. mortgage market:** ~5-6 million originations per year.
- **Total U.S. adults considering home purchase in next 3 years:** ~50-70 million (per various surveys).
- **Annual "qualification curiosity" searches:** 10-50 million (per Google keyword data).
- **Denied applicants per year:** ~500K-1M (per Fed denial rate × application volume).
- **Addressable market for educational diagnostic:** 10-30 million visits/year realistically achievable with SEO.

### Consumer demand signals
- **"Why was I denied" searches are rising.**
- **"How much can I qualify for" is consistently top-10 mortgage search.**
- **NerdWallet, Bankrate, Rocket all get massive traffic to calculator tools.**
- **No site has nailed the "personalized diagnosis" angle.**

## 1.12 Borrower Behavior

### Mortgage shopping journey (per NAR / MBA / industry data)
- **Pre-shopping research:** 2-6 months for first-time buyers.
- **Number of lenders contacted:** 1-3 average; 4+ for savvy shoppers.
- **Channels used:** Google, lender websites, real estate agents, recommendations.
- **Most trusted sources:** Real estate agents, then lender websites, then financial media.
- **Prequalification vs. preapproval:** Many borrowers don't know the difference.

### Lead generation trends
- **Aggregator leads (LendingTree, etc.):** Low conversion (1-2% to funded), high volume.
- **Direct lender leads:** Higher conversion (3-5% to funded), more expensive.
- **Organic / content leads:** Highest quality, longest to build.
- **Referral / SOI leads:** Best quality, but slow to scale.

### Mobile vs. desktop
- **Mobile:** ~60-70% of mortgage searches (Google).
- **Application completion:** Still skewed to desktop, but mobile is rising.
- **Site must be mobile-first.**

## 1.13 First-Time Buyer Specifics

### Demographics
- **Median age:** 36 (record high).
- **Median income:** ~$80K.
- **Median down payment:** 6-9% (was 20%+ historically).
- **Biggest barriers:** Down payment, credit, debt, lack of knowledge.

### Programs they don't know about
- State HFA programs (CalHFA, FL HFA, OHFA, etc.)
- FHA (3.5% down, 580 FICO)
- VA (0% down for veterans)
- USDA (0% down in rural areas)
- Mortgage Credit Certificates (federal tax credit)
- State down payment assistance
- Employer-assisted housing
- Good Neighbor Next Door (HUD)
- Path Ready Buyer (Fannie Mae)

### Education gap
- **Many don't know these programs exist.**
- **Even when they do, the application process is confusing.**
- **This is a key content opportunity for the diagnostic site.**

## 1.14 Mortgage Insurance Confusion

### The issue
- Many consumers don't know about:
  - PMI (private mortgage insurance for conventional <20% down)
  - MIP (mortgage insurance premium for FHA, often for life of loan)
  - Funding fee for VA loans
  - USDA guarantee fee
- They see a "5% down" loan and don't realize it costs $X/month more.

### Impact on qualification
- PMI/MIP adds to PITI, which affects DTI.
- For FHA: can add $200-400/month to a $300K loan.
- This is a huge affordability issue often missed.

## 1.15 Self-Employed Specifics

### The hidden pain
- Self-employed borrowers are 2-3x more likely to be denied than W-2 (per Urban Institute).
- Most have "enough" income but not on paper.
- They get frustrated by calculators that ask for "gross income" but lenders use "net."

### Loan options for self-employed
- **Bank statement:** 12-24 months of business deposits. Higher rates, larger down.
- **DSCR:** For investors. Based on property cash flow, not personal income.
- **Asset depletion:** For high net worth. Liquid assets / time = qualifying income.
- **P&L only:** Some lenders accept YTD P&L + 1 prior return.
- **1099:** For independent contractors with W-9 income.
- **Co-borrower:** Use a W-2 spouse/partner.

### Documentation burden
- 2 years personal + business tax returns
- YTD P&L
- Business bank statements
- Business license
- Sometimes CPA letter
- Sometimes K-1s

## 1.16 Documentation Problems (Cross-Cutting)

### Common documentation issues
- **Large deposits:** Source required (typically >50% of monthly income).
- **Gift funds:** Letter required from donor.
- **Rental income:** 75% of gross, with vacancy factor.
- **Alimony/child support:** Court order + 3-year receipt history.
- **Investment income:** 2-year average + likely continuation.
- **Foreign income:** Complex — W-8BEN, foreign tax returns, sometimes not eligible.
- **Retirement income:** Award letter + 1099-R.
- **Disability income:** Award letter + likely continuation.

### The "what documents do I need?" question
- Consumers don't know what to gather.
- This is a key content + diagnostic opportunity.

## 1.17 Property-Specific Problems

### Condo challenges
- **FHA condo approval** — only approved projects eligible.
- **HOA cert** — required, can take 2-4 weeks.
- **Litigation** — projects in active litigation are ineligible.
- **Commercial concentration** — >25% commercial = problems.
- **Insurance** — HO-6 + master policy required.

### Insurance issues
- **Wildfire zones (CA, OR, CO):** Many insurers pulling out.
- **Hurricane zones (FL, TX, NC, LA):** High premiums, deductibles.
- **Flood zones:** Required flood insurance, expensive.
- **Coastal areas:** Wind pool / beach plan required.

### Manufactured homes
- Must be on permanent foundation.
- Built after June 15, 1976 (HUD code).
- Some loan types don't allow (VA has restrictions).
- Often requires specific lenders.

## 1.18 Recent CFPB / Regulatory Trends

### Key 2023-2025 actions
- **CFPB December 2024 interpretive rule on digital mortgage comparison tools** — clarifies MAP Rule, Reg Z, and Reg B apply to comparison sites.
- **April 2023 CFPB/DOJ/FTC/EEOC Joint Statement on AI** — agencies coordinating on AI enforcement.
- **2024 redlining settlements** (CrossCountry, Fairway, etc.) — fair lending enforcement active.
- **Insurance Marketing Coalition v. FCC (11th Cir., Jan 2025)** — vacated the one-to-one consent rule for TCPA, but lead generators still face other consent requirements.
- **Colorado AI Act (effective Feb 1, 2026)** — high-risk AI obligations, may apply to mortgage qualification AI.

### Implications
- Mortgage lead generation is one of the most heavily regulated industries in the U.S.
- AI in mortgage decisions is under active regulatory scrutiny.
- "Prequalification" vs. "preapproval" distinction is critical.
- Truthful advertising is enforced.

## 1.19 Key Market Opportunities for the Diagnostic

### Opportunity 1: Anonymous diagnosis
- **No one offers this.** All competitors require SSN + DOB.
- **Consumer demand:** High — surveys show 70%+ of consumers are uncomfortable sharing SSN before knowing what they'll get.
- **Compliance:** Easier than full application — no credit pull, no FCRA, less ECOA exposure.

### Opportunity 2: Self-employed diagnosis
- **Self-employed borrowers are systematically underserved.**
- They have a specific, acute need for tools that understand their situation.
- **Lower competition for SEO terms like "self-employed mortgage qualification."**

### Opportunity 3: Educational diagnosis (not just "what do I qualify for")
- Most calculators answer a number.
- Few tools answer the educational question: "WHY can't I qualify?"
- **This is the brand's core differentiator.**

### Opportunity 4: Scenario simulation
- "What if I paid off $X in credit card debt?"
- "What if I improved my credit to 720?"
- "What if I saved for 6 more months?"
- **No major competitor does this.**

### Opportunity 5: Cross-program routing
- "You don't qualify for conventional, but FHA might work."
- "VA loans exist if you're a veteran."
- "Bank statement loans exist if you're self-employed."
- **No major competitor surfaces alternative programs when borrower fails the first check.**

### Opportunity 6: Denied borrower follow-up
- "You were denied. Here's what was likely the issue. Here's what to do next."
- **No major competitor serves this audience well.**
