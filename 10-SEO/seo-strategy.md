# PART 10 — SEO STRATEGY

## 10.1 SEO Philosophy: Be the Answer

The goal is to **own the search results for "why can't I qualify for a mortgage"** and adjacent high-intent queries. This means:

1. **Be the most helpful, most accurate, most honest answer** to mortgage qualification questions
2. **Match search intent precisely** — informational content for informational queries, diagnostic for commercial queries
3. **Build topical authority** — comprehensive coverage of mortgage qualification topics
4. **Earn backlinks** through genuinely useful content (and partnerships)
5. **Optimize for AI search** (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews) — increasingly important

## 10.6 On-Page SEO Standards

### Title Tag Template
`[Primary Keyword] | [Benefit] | [Brand]`
- E.g., "Why Can't I Qualify for a Mortgage? | Free 5-Minute Check | [Brand]"
- Under 60 characters
- Primary keyword near front

### Meta Description Template
`[Hook] [Benefit]. [Proof]. [CTA].`
- E.g., "Not sure why you can't qualify? Take our free 5-minute mortgage readiness check — no credit pull, no SSN. See what may be holding you back. Get my free snapshot →"
- 150-160 characters
- Action-oriented, includes keyword

### H1 / H2 / H3 Structure
- One H1 per page (primary keyword)
- H2s for major sections (secondary keywords)
- H3s for sub-sections (long-tail variations)

### Internal Linking
- Every article links to: the diagnostic, 2-3 related articles, 1 state page (if applicable)
- Every state page links to: the diagnostic, national guide, related articles
- Hub-and-spoke architecture with pillar pages at the center

### Schema Markup
- **FAQPage** schema on FAQ-rich articles
- **HowTo** schema on guides with steps
- **Article** schema on all blog posts
- **Organization** schema on homepage
- **LocalBusiness** schema on about/contact
- **FinancialProduct** schema (carefully — see compliance notes) for program pages
- **BreadcrumbList** on all pages
- **WebSite** with SearchAction for sitelinks search box

### URL Structure
- `/blog/[slug]`
- `/[state]/[topic]` (state pages)
- `/resources/[slug]`
- `/about`
- `/privacy-policy`
- `/terms`
- `/contact`
- Avoid: dates, query strings, unnecessary parameters

### Content Standards
- Flesch reading ease 60+ (consumer-friendly)
- 8th-grade reading level
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bullets for lists
- Tables for comparisons
- Images with alt text
- Internal links in every 500-800 words
- CTA every 800-1,200 words
- Mobile-first

## 10.13 Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Google Helpful Content Update (HCU) | Focus on original, helpful content. Avoid thin content. |
| AI content detection | Use AI to draft, human to edit. Original insight. |
| Google spam update | No doorway pages, no spun content, no PBNs. |
| High competition for big keywords | Focus on long-tail + local. Own specific topics. |
| Slow organic growth | Combine with paid + partnerships. |
| Algorithm changes | Diversify traffic sources. Build brand, not just SEO. |

## 10.14 SEO Compliance Notes

- **No misleading titles** — "guaranteed approval" in title is a mortgage advertising violation
- **No fake urgency in titles** — "rates going up today" is regulated
- **No NMLS violations** — NMLS ID must be in ads that imply licensure
- **Disclosures in copy** — equal housing, licensing, etc.
- **Avoid black hat** — no cloaking, no doorway pages, no spam
- **Quality > Quantity** — one great article > 10 thin ones
