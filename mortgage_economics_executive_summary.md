### 2.2 Speed-to-Contact — The Single Biggest Lever

| Response Time | Conversion Impact |
|---|---|
| Within 1 minute | **391% higher conversion** (MIT/InsideSales study) |
| Within 5 minutes | **21x more likely** to convert vs. 30 minutes (Insellerate/MBA study) |
| After 30 minutes | **~100x worse** than 5-minute response |
| 40% of new leads | **Never contacted at all** (Insellerate) |
| 78% of customers | Buy from the **first responder** (Lead Connect) |

| Paid Search | 40–50% | 25–30% | **2–4%** |

| Referral | 70–80% | 50–60% | **8–15%** |
| Facebook/Meta | 25–35% | 15–20% | **0.5–2%** |
| Aged leads | 20–30% | 10–18% | **1–3%** |

### 2.4 Purchase vs. Refinance

- **Purchase lead-to-funded: 3–5%** (life-driven, higher intent)
- **Refi lead-to-funded: 1–2%** in elevated-rate environments (rate-sensitive, comparison shopping)
- Don't blend them — they have fundamentally different economics.

### 2.5 Multi-Step vs. Single-Step Form

- **Multi-step forms** (2–3 steps, progress bar) increase form completion 20–40% vs. single long forms
- **Single-step forms** generate higher-quality leads but at lower volume
- Recommended for solo MLO: 2–3 step form (loan purpose/amount → property/employment → contact)

### 2.6 Inbound Call Conversion

| Stage | Paid Leads | Referral Leads |
|---|---|---|
| Call → completed application | 15–25% | 30–45% |
| Call → scheduled appointment | 20–35% | 40–60% |
| Call → funded loan | 10–20% | 25–35% |

### 3.1 The ROI Threshold

Assuming:
- $400K average loan
- 100 bps gross commission = $4,000
- $500 processing + $595 company fee + $200 tech = $1,295 in costs
- **Net per funded loan (before lead cost) = $2,705**

**Break-even cost per funded loan ≈ $2,705** — anything above this is unprofitable on direct math.

**Therefore**: every channel with cost-per-funded-loan **above ~$2,500** must be balanced by higher-margin loans (jumbo, HELOC, non-QM) or by referral volume that subsidizes paid acquisition.

### 3.2 Top Channel ROI Findings

1. **Referrals and SOI dominate** with cost per funded loan of $200–$1,000 and 8–15% conversion. This is why mature MLOs (5+ years) run 70%+ referral business. Real estate agent partnerships are the single highest-ROI activity for purchase MLOs.

2. **SEO is the second-best ROI channel** at $300–$1,500 cost per funded loan, but requires 6–18 months of investment before producing meaningful volume. Best for solo MLOs who can invest time in content.

3. **Google Search (non-branded) is the highest-quality paid channel** at $2,000–$10,000 cost per funded loan. Operational excellence (sub-5-minute response, persistent follow-up) is the differentiator between a $2,000 CPA and a $10,000 CPA on identical CPL.

4. **Facebook/Meta has the worst direct ROI** for solo MLOs because of Special Ad Category restrictions (no lookalikes, no PII retargeting, no interest-based targeting). Best used for brand awareness + retargeting via email/SMS, not direct response.

5. **Zillow Custom Quotes is no longer the easy lead source** it was in 2019–2021. Zillow Home Loans is now originating the loans itself (Q2 2025: $1.1B+ purchase volume, +50% YoY). Third-party lenders in Custom Quotes compete with Zillow's own lender for the same borrower.

6. **LendingTree and home equity products are the standout growth segment** for 2026. LendingTree's home equity revenue grew 38% YoY in Q2 2025 to $30.3M; Home segment overall +25% YoY to $40.4M. Solo MLOs should consider adding HELOC/home equity to their product mix.

### 3.3 The Compounding Math

A 1% improvement in lead-to-funded conversion at $75 CPL:
- Old: $75 ÷ 0.02 = $3,750 cost per funded loan
- New: $75 ÷ 0.03 = $2,500 cost per funded loan
- **$1,250 savings per funded loan**
- Over 50 funded loans/year = $62,500 more take-home

A 1-minute improvement in response time can move conversion 30–50%. That single operational change can transform the unit economics for any operation spending >$2K/mo on paid.

## 5. Industry Context (2024–2026)

- **30-year fixed rate**: 6.66% (Freddie Mac PMMS, week of Aug 20, 2026); 6.84% (HousingWire live, Aug 27, 2026)
- **Projected to stay 6.0–6.8% through 2026** — "rate plateau" environment, not a refi boom
- **Purchase/refi mix**: ~70–75% purchase, 25–30% refi
- **MBA Q3 2025 production cost per loan**: $11,109 (up from $10,965 Q2)
- **IMB Q3 2025 profit**: $370/loan (3 bp net margin — razor-thin)
- **LendingTree Q2 2025**: Home segment $40.4M (+25% YoY), home equity $30.3M (+38% YoY)
- **Zillow Home Loans Q2 2025**: >$1.1B purchase volume (+50% YoY)
- **Redfin Mortgage**: shut down March 2023; no more Redfin-branded leads
- **AI adoption**: 38% of lenders use AI/ML (STRATMOR 2024 Tech Insight); AI trust fell 30%→16% in one year (Cotality 2026)

## 6. Top 5 Recommendations

1. **Build organic/referral first** — 50%+ of funded loans from non-paid sources by month 6.
2. **Sub-5-minute response time** — the highest-ROI investment you can make. AI chatbot + SMS auto-responder + auto-dialer. Pays back in <60 days at any spend level >$2K/mo.
3. **Pick 1–2 paid channels** (Google Search + one aggregator) and master them. Multi-channel dilution kills solo ops.
4. **Track cost per funded loan, not CPL** — CPL is vanity. Cut any source producing >$4,000 per funded loan.
5. **Add home equity to your product mix** — it's the only segment showing real growth (+38% YoY at LendingTree Q2 2025) and has the most favorable rate environment dynamics (homeowners with sub-4% first mortgages need cash via HELOC, not refi).
