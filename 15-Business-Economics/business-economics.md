## 15.4 Revenue Per Funded Loan

### Average loan size (2024-2026)
- National median: ~$420,000
- Median first-time buyer: ~$340,000
- High-cost areas: $500k+
- Varies by state

### Revenue per loan (assuming MLO commission)

#### Independent mortgage broker (origination fee model)
- Origination fee: 1-2% of loan amount
- Yield spread premium (YSP): 0-2% depending on rate
- Broker fee: 1-2.5% all-in
- **Average per loan: 2.0-2.75% × $400k = $8,000-11,000**

#### Retail MLO (commission model)
- Base salary: $40-60k
- Commission per funded loan: 30-80 bps
- Plus bonus structure
- **Average per loan: 0.50-0.80% × $400k = $2,000-3,200**

#### Hybrid (small brokerage, partial commission)
- Origination + lender-paid comp
- **Average per loan: 1.0-1.5% × $400k = $4,000-6,000**

**Assumed for this model: $5,000 per funded loan** (blend)

## 15.6 Break-Even Analysis

### Variables
- Marketing spend: $M/month
- Visitors: $V (depending on CPC and budget)
- Conversion rate: depends on funnel
- Revenue per funded loan: $R
- Fixed costs: $F/month (hosting, tools, etc.)

### Break-even formula
**Funded loans/month × Revenue per loan = Marketing + Fixed costs**

### Conservative break-even
- 0.5 funded loans/month
- $5,000 revenue/loan
- = $2,500/month revenue
- Marketing + fixed must be ≤ $2,500/month
- **Break-even: ~$1,500/month marketing + $1,000 fixed**

### Expected break-even
- 0.8 funded loans/month
- $5,000 revenue/loan
- = $4,000/month revenue
- **Break-even: ~$2,500/month marketing + $1,500 fixed**

### Excellent break-even
- 4.4 funded loans/month
- = $22,000/month revenue
- **Break-even: $20,000/month marketing + $1,500 fixed**

**The smallest leverage points:**

2. App → funded (depends on lender, not you)

## 15.10 Comparison to Alternatives

### Alternative 1: Just buy leads
- Cost: $50-300 per lead
- Quality: highly variable
- Conversion to funded: 1-3%
- **Cost per funded loan: $1,500-30,000** (massively variable)

### Alternative 2: Build a different lead-gen site
- Cost: similar
- Conversion: depends on concept
- **Cost per funded loan: similar range**

### Alternative 3: Sphere of influence / past clients only
- Cost: time, low $
- Conversion: 5-15% (warm leads)
- **Cost per funded loan: $50-200**
- **BUT:** requires 200+ SOI relationships, multi-year trust building

### Alternative 4: Real estate agent referrals
- Cost: 0% (give commission split)
- Conversion: 20-40% (warm)
- **Cost per funded loan: 0% to 1% (split)**

## 15.11 Why This Concept Has Better Unit Economics Than Lead Buying

| Factor | This concept | Lead buying |
|---|---|---|
| Brand value | Compounds over time | None |
| SEO moat | Yes (high-quality content) | None |
| Cost per visitor | $1-3 (paid) or $0 (organic) | $50-300 per lead (CPL) |
| Trust | High (educational, no pressure) | Low (inbound lead, often shared) |
| Compliance | Self-controlled | Dependent on lead vendor |
| Long-term value | Yes (email list compounds) | No (one-shot) |

**The "build vs buy" math strongly favors building when:**
- You have time to invest
- You're in it for the long term
- You have a differentiated concept
- You want brand value

## 15.13 Cash Flow Considerations

### Lag time: significant
- Visitor → funded loan: 30-120 days
- Revenue: realized at closing
- Marketing: paid monthly
- Costs: paid monthly

### Implication
- Plan for **3-6 months of negative cash flow** before break-even
- Have $10,000-20,000 in reserve
- Don't blow the entire budget in month 1

### Worst case (year 1)
- $40,000 marketing + $15,000 fixed = $55,000
- Some months may be -$5,000
- Cumulative cash: -$15,000 to -$30,000 before turning positive
- **Cash buffer required: $30,000-50,000**
